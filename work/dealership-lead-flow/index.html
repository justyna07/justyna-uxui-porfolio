Case study 1 / Automotive · Conversion UX

From abandoned forms to a clearer path to qualified leads.

A conversion-focused mobile inquiry flow designed around intent, clarity and the moment customers are asked to share their contact details.

RoleLead UX concept · Analytics
MethodsFunnel review · Prototyping · Usability
ToolsFigma · GA4 · GTM · Call tracking
01 / Context

The click was working. The page wasn’t.

Paid-search traffic to a dealership quote page was holding steady, while lead efficiency deteriorated. The mobile form required seven fields and asked for contact information before communicating what a shopper would receive.

The research direction combined GA4 funnel exploration, call-quality review and usability sessions to understand both abandonment and downstream lead quality.

Problem: Make the mobile quote path easier to complete without sacrificing the purchase-intent context that business development reps need.

02 / Constraints

Real constraints shape the solution.

  • Third-party dealership CMS and embedded components.
  • Consent language subject to legal approval.
  • Existing BDC lead routing and callback process.
  • GA4 and Google Ads conversion tracking needed to remain consistent during testing.
03 / Decision

Qualify first. Ask for contact last.

Three concepts were evaluated: a shorter one-page form, a three-step intent-first form, and a call-only page. The stepped design balanced the needs of after-hours shoppers, sales teams and lead quality.

Option A · Trim the form

Lower implementation cost, but still asked for contact details too early.

Option B · Intent-first flow
Selected concept

Vehicle → timing → contact. Clear value proposition and an optional call path.

Option C · Call-only

Direct for ready-to-buy customers, but excludes many after-hours leads.

05 / Outcome

Designed for measurable lead quality.

The handoff design specified form-step events, field errors, click-to-call placement and lead-quality values that could be reconciled with CRM data. No measured post-launch lift is published here because verified results have not been supplied.

Primary measurement

Mobile form completion rate and cost per qualified lead.

Quality measurement

BDC-qualified call rate, purchase timing and downstream appointments.

06 / Reflection

What I would improve.

Recruit first-time dealership shoppers rather than relying only on a store customer list; define a more complete step-level baseline before launch; and test softer labels for shoppers who are still researching.
