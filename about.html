Designer with a performance-marketing spine.

I came to product design from the side that pays for the traffic. Since 2022 I've run paid media for local-service businesses: 14 markets and a $150K monthly budget at Life Storage, 50 to 100 dealership accounts at Force Marketing, and now insurance and automotive lead-gen at Melon Local, where most of my time goes into building AI-assisted systems for auditing, search-term analysis and reporting. That work taught me exactly where good design makes a measurable difference, because I was the one paying for every visitor who left.

The design side isn't new. I trained in UX/UI at Thinkful in 2022 and spent a year designing and optimising the UX and UI of Henry Meds' digital properties alongside the growth work. The three case studies on this site are where the two halves meet.

What I bring to a product team

  • Discovery that starts in the data: funnels, search terms, call recordings, cost per lead. Interviews explain the why; the numbers say where to look.
  • A working UX practice: research, flows, design systems in Figma, usability testing, and handoff specs a vendor developer can build from without a meeting.
  • Hands-on AI automation. I build the pipelines (verified inputs, structured output, human review before anything ships) and design the interfaces that make them trustworthy. Current side project: an AI PPC copilot that reads account data, finds issues and ranks what to fix.
  • Tracking shipped with every design (GA4, Google Tag Manager, call tracking), so impact is never a guess.
  • Client-facing from day one: I've been the primary point of contact for multi-account clients, so I know what a stakeholder needs to see to say yes.
