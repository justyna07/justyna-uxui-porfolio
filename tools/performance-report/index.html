How this works

Every Monday the pipeline pulls last week's Google Ads and GA4 numbers, flags anomalies against the prior four weeks, and drafts a plain-English summary for the client. Nothing is sent until the account manager reads it, edits it, and approves. Every sentence with a number is checked against the data it came from; a sentence the data can't support blocks sending.
