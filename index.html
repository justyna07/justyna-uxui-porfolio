How I work

Every project runs the same loop: discover from quantitative data first, define a testable problem with a metric attached, design options rather than a single answer, validate with real users before build, hand off with a tracking plan, then measure and go again.

Where AI fits: synthesis, first drafts, variants and reporting. Where it doesn't: choosing the problem, choosing the option, and signing off anything a customer sees.
